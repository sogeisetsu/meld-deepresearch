# Design-level scores — per-axis justification

Scored by reading each skill's own files. No live run. `meld-deepresearch` was
read from this repository's `skills/meld-deepresearch/`; the other two from their
public repositories (see the librarian dossier in section 4 of
[`../README.md`](../README.md)).

## `meld-deepresearch` (ours) — 30 / 35

| Axis | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| A Coverage | 4 | `normal` names non-overlapping axes + `plan.json`; `--plan` gate fails an uncovered axis (`E_PLAN_DIM_UNCOVERED`). Capped at 4 because the two-tier budget (25 fetches / 15 sources) can end broad topics early by design. |
| B Depth | 4 | Per-axis loop with a depth threshold; `key_findings` is a derived synthesis layer. No dedicated multi-pass "deepen" role like SenseNova's review/supplement. |
| C Factual support | 5 | Enforced: `factual` needs a `primary`/`secondary` source or an observation (`E_FACTUAL_SOURCE`); snippets are never evidence; `tertiary` alone fails. |
| D Citation quality | 5 | `render_citations.py` gate fails on orphan / unresolved markers; `dedupe_sources.py` normalizes URLs; interpretive claims need two distinct origins (`E_INTERPRETIVE_TWO`). |
| E Instruction-following | 4 | Language/format/output-dir anchors, tier override honored, clarify questions scope-only. |
| F Presentation | 3 | Markdown deliverable only. No HTML/chart rendering (deliberate: rules 2 and 8). Readable, but no visual deliverable. |
| G Process discipline | 5 | Forced refutation (`W_NO_REFUTE`), explicit budgets/stop rules, `observations[]` with a re-runnable command, two fail-closed gates, degradation honesty (§9). |

## Reading the totals

The totals are close between ours and SenseNova (30 vs 29) but for **different
reasons**: we win C/D/G on enforced discipline; SenseNova wins F on the
visual deliverable and matches us structurally on breadth. Weizhena trails on
everything downstream of retrieval because it has no evidence contract.
