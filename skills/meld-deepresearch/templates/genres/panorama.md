# Genre: Panorama (全景 / 趋势)

Appends the following sections **after** `## Findings`, before the
renderer-owned `## Sources`. Use for a landscape, market or trend survey where
breadth across one field is the point.

1. **Background & current state** — what the field is and where it stands now,
   in the report language (背景与现状). May draw on `background` claims.
2. **Core dimension 1..N** — one section per major dimension the plan named
   (players, funding, cost structure, technology, regulation...). Each carries
   inline citations like `## Findings`.
3. **Synthesis** — how the dimensions fit together (综合分析), not a repeat of
   the per-dimension sections.
4. **Outlook** — forward-looking, marked as projection (`kind: projective`) and
   never stated as fact (结论与展望).
5. **Appendix** — optional tables, glossaries or method notes (附录).

Required sections (`Executive Summary` / `Findings` / `Contradictions &
Counter-evidence` / `Gaps & Unknowns`) are unchanged and always come first.
