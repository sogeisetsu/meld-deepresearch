# Genre: Entity (实体调查 / 尽调)

Appends the following sections **after** `## Findings`, before the
renderer-owned `## Sources`. Use for due diligence, investment or a
person/organisation background check.

1. **Subject profile** — who or what the subject is (主体画像).
2. **Operations** — what the subject actually does; products, market, model
   (业务 / 运营).
3. **Financials** — revenue, funding, cost, runway, with date-stamped figures
   (财务). Every number carries a citation.
4. **Legal / compliance** — filings, litigation, regulatory status (法律 / 合规).
5. **Team** — key people, roles, track record (团队).
6. **Red flags** — unresolved risks, contradictions and anything that fails the
   refutation pass (红旗事项). This section may be short but should not be
   omitted when a concern exists.
7. **Conclusion & recommendation** — the read on the subject (结论与建议).

Sub-structure is chosen by purpose: checklist-style for due diligence,
argument-driven for an investment case, background-style for a person or
organisation. Required sections are unchanged and always come first.
